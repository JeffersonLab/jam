Expirations and notifications¶

User Guide › Expirations and notifications

Every permission the director gives, and every Verified or Provisionally Verified control, has an expiration date. JAM checks for expirations, revokes what has expired, and warns people a week ahead.

When JAM checks¶

  • Every night at midnight, for every facility: JAM revokes what has expired, then sends the upcoming expiration notices.
  • Whenever someone opens a facility's page on the Authorizations tab: JAM revokes what has expired in that facility, so the page never shows an expired permission as current.

Something that expires during the day is therefore revoked when the facility's page is next opened, or at midnight, whichever comes first.

What expiration does¶

  1. Each Verified or Provisionally Verified control whose expiration has passed becomes Not Verified, with the comment "Expired". Its history gets a row modified by jam-admin.
  2. In the facility's current authorization, JAM sets to high power RF No, or beam mode None:
  3. each permission whose expiration has passed, with the comment "Permission automatically revoked due to director's authorization expiration"; and
  4. each permission of a segment or destination with a control that just expired, with the comment "Permission automatically revoked due to credited control control verification expiration".
  5. If any permission was revoked, JAM saves the result as a new authorization: its change notes list the segments or destinations revoked, it keeps the last authorizer's signature, and it is marked "Automatically reduced". Then JAM posts a log entry and sends emails, as below.

A downgrade by a verification team revokes permission in the same way, at once, with the comment "Permission automatically revoked due to credited control control verification downgrade".

If a control expires or is downgraded where there was no permission to revoke, only the verification changes: there is no new authorization, log entry or email.

Emails and log entries¶

Each change to an authorization, whether by an authorizer or automatic, gets a log entry and emails with a picture of the new authorization. The log entry goes to the facility's logbooks (Inventory › Facilities), tagged Readme, titled "JAM: Facility RF Authorization Updated" or "JAM: Facility Beam Authorization Updated", and posted as the authorizer, or, for an automatic reduction, as the last authorizer. The email subjects start with "JAM: Facility".

Event Log entry Watchers Administrators and facility manager Verification team
An authorizer saves an authorization Yes Yes Yes No
A downgrade revokes permission Yes Yes Yes The downgraded control's team: "… Authorization Reduced due to Control Verification Downgrade"
An expiration revokes permission Yes Yes Yes The team of each expired control: "… Authorization Reduced due to Control Verification Expiration"
Upcoming expirations (nightly) No No Yes Yes, for the team's controls

Watchers get the RF or beam emails they are listed for (Inventory › Watchers). The administrators are the users with the jam-admin role, and the verification team is the users with the team's directory role.

Log entries and emails are made in the background after the change, and a failure doesn't undo the change. Always check JAM for the current authorization, as each log entry says.

Upcoming expiration notices¶

Each night JAM emails "JAM: Facility Upcoming Expiration Notice" about what will expire between six and seven days later, so each expiration is normally announced once, a week ahead:

  • to the administrators and the facility's manager: the facility's RF and beam permissions and control verifications that will expire;
  • to each verification team: its control verifications that will expire.

The Expiring link on the Verifications tab lists everything expiring within the next seven days, and the Authorizations and Verifications pages mark it (Expiring Soon).

To keep an operation running, verify its controls again, or give a new permission, before it expires.
