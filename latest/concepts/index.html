Concepts¶

User Guide › Concepts

Facilities and operations¶

A facility is a machine or test area JAM authorizes, such as CEBAF, LERF, UITF, CMTF or VTA. Each facility has a page on the Authorizations tab, a manager who is emailed about it, and the logbooks JAM posts its changes to.

JAM authorizes two operations types in each facility, separately:

  • RF operations, by RF segment: a part of the facility that runs RF, such as CEBAF's Injector, North Linac and South Linac, or a whole test facility ("Entire Facility"). The permission is whether the segment may run high power RF.
  • Beam operations, by beam destination: a dump, hall or other place beam may be sent, such as Hall A or the Inline Dump. The permission is a beam mode and a current limit.

A facility without segments or destinations has no panel for that type; VTA and CMTF, for example, have RF segments but no beam destinations.

Beam modes¶

The beam modes depend on the facility:

Facility Beam modes
CEBAF None, Tune, CW
LERF None, Ceramic Viewer, Viewer Limited, High Duty Cycle, BLM Checkout, CW
UITF None, Viewer Limited, Tune, CW

None means no beam. Each destination has its own current units, normally µA (uA). A destination with a beam mode but no current limit is Dump Power Limited: its current is limited only by the power its dump can take.

Credited controls¶

A credited control is an engineered or administrative control that the safety envelope (ASE) credits for safe operation: shielding, Personnel Safety System (PSS) access and beam containment controls, oxygen deficiency hazard (ODH) controls, barriers and so on. Each control has a description, links to its documents, a verification frequency (how often it must be checked, or "As Needed"), and one responsible verification team.

A control participates in an operation when it is required for that RF segment or beam destination. Each pairing of a control with a segment or destination has its own verification: a status, when and by whom it was verified, when it expires, comments, an optional documentation link and, optionally, the components it covers.

Verification status¶

Status Icon Meaning
Verified Green thumbs-up The team has checked the control for this operation, until the expiration date.
Provisionally Verified Yellow warning triangle The control is accepted with conditions. Permission is still possible, but the authorizer must explain it in a comment.
Not Verified Red X The control isn't verified, or its verification expired or was downgraded.

A segment's or destination's status is the worst status of the controls that participate in it, and its expiration is the earliest of their expirations. A segment or destination with no participating controls counts as Verified. A facility's status for a control is likewise the worst of that control's verifications in the facility.

The director's permission¶

The Director of Operations, or a delegate (an authorizer), gives each facility's authorization: for each RF segment, high power RF Yes or No, and for each beam destination, a beam mode and current limit. Each permission other than None or No has an expiration date and may have a comment. Each save records a new authorization, with the change notes, who authorized it and when, so the history keeps every version.

Approved operations¶

An RF segment or beam destination is approved, shown by a green thumbs-up in the Auth column of its facility's page, only while both:

  1. the director's permission allows it (high power RF is Yes, or the beam mode isn't None), and
  2. its credited controls are Verified or Provisionally Verified.

Operators must honor the permission as shown, and stay within its beam mode and current limit.

Expiration and automatic reduction¶

Permissions and verifications expire. When a verification expires, JAM sets it to Not Verified, and when a team downgrades one, JAM keeps the team's new status; either way, JAM sets the permission of each segment or destination it affects to No or None. When a permission's own expiration date passes, JAM sets it to No or None too. These automatic reductions are saved as a new authorization, still signed by the last authorizer and marked "Automatically reduced", and are announced by email and log entry. JAM never raises a permission by itself: restoring it takes an authorizer.

Anything expiring within seven days is marked (Expiring Soon). See Expirations and notifications.

Roles¶

Role Who Can
Viewer Anyone, logged in or not See every page except Setup.
Operator Operations staff Read and honor the authorizations. Operators need no special role.
Authorizer Users listed as a facility's authorizers for RF or beam (Inventory › Authorizers) Change that facility's RF or beam authorization.
Verification team member Users with the team's directory role (Inventory › Verifiers) Verify, or downgrade, the controls their team is responsible for. Gets the team's emails.
Watcher Users listed as a facility's watchers for RF or beam (Inventory › Watchers) Gets an email each time that authorization changes.
Facility manager The manager of a facility (Inventory › Facilities) Gets the facility's change and expiration emails.
Administrator Users with the jam-admin role Do everything above for every facility and team, edit the inventory, and use the Setup tab.

Roles come from JLab's user directory (Keycloak); the authorizer, watcher and manager lists are kept in JAM.
